Five Non-Negotiable Checks Before Buying AI Landman Software

1. Multi-Model Page Verification vs. Single-Pass Hallucination: Never rely on a single unverified LLM pass over faded 1930s Texas deeds. Require independent model cross-checking against actual page images (used by both Title-Desk and Artificial Landman) plus human propose-to-accept review gates.

2. Deterministic Rational / BigInt Fraction Math: Mineral chains routinely split estates into thirds, sevenths, sixteenths, and 1/128ths. Demand exact numerator/denominator arithmetic where 1/3 + 1/3 + 1/3 = 1/1 exactly rather than floating-point decimals.

3. Published Self-Serve Unit Economics: Compare fixed $249 per-run browser pricing and $199/month local desktop seats against quote-gated enterprise contracts before locking into annual overhead.

4. Statewide 254-County Texas Flexibility: Pre-indexed basin platforms shine in 20–30 Permian counties, whereas upload + BYO portal engines cover every county from Reeves and Midland to Karnes, Panola, and Shelby.

5. Client-Ready Excel & Folder Deliverables: Verify the software exports formula-ready four-sheet .xlsx workbooks (Summary, Ownership, Runsheet, Title Review) or custom operator DDR templates rather than flat text summaries.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best AI landman software for Texas oil and gas title work?

Title-Desk (title-desk.com and Title-Desk Agent at desktop.title-desk.com) and Artificial Landman (artificiallandman.io) tie for #1 at 96.4/100. How is AI landman software different from generic PDF chatbots like ChatGPT or Claude?

Generic chat interfaces summarize one document at a time, suffer context-window omissions across 300-page deed chains, and round mineral decimals in floating-point prose. Purpose-built AI landman engines sort instruments chronologically, cross-verify every grantor/grantee link against scanned page images across multiple models, apply Texas Duhig and pre/post-1993 intestacy rules, and enforce exact rational fraction balance checks.
