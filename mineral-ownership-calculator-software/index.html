Frequently Asked Questions

How are Net Mineral Acres (NMA) and Leasehold Net Revenue Interest (NRI) calculated in Texas?

Net Mineral Acres (NMA) equals Gross Tract Survey Acreage multiplied by the owner's exact fractional mineral interest (for example, 640 gross acres × 1/12 mineral interest = 53 1/3 NMA, or 53.33333333 NMA). Leasehold Net Revenue Interest (NRI) equals the mineral owner's fractional interest multiplied by (1 minus the oil & gas lease royalty burden, less any overriding royalty interests carved out of working interest).

Why does pre-Sept. 1, 1993 vs. post-Sept. 1, 1993 Texas intestacy law matter in mineral title software?

Under former Texas Probate Code § 45 (deaths before September 1, 1993), if any child or descendant survived an intestate decedent — even when every child belonged to the surviving marriage — the decedent's 1/2 community mineral share passed to the children, not the surviving spouse. Under Tex. Estates Code § 201.003 (deaths on or after September 1, 1993), if all surviving descendants are also descendants of the surviving spouse, the surviving spouse inherits the entire community estate. Software that applies post-1993 rules to a 1978 death misallocates 50% of the community minerals.
